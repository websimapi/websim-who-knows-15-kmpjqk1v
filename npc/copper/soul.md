# Nessa Calder

Nessa runs a repair shop where people bring jammed locks, broken cases, and old keys. She was contracted to replace the mansion's failing door hardware before the property sale. When she arrived, her invoice had been signed by someone using the name of a person she has never met. She stayed to get paid and to learn who had ordered access to the house. Her knowledge of locks gives her opportunities, but no automatic knowledge of who used a door tonight.

**Perspective.** Nessa sees mechanical cause before dramatic motive. She notices scratches near a latch, a door that swings against its frame, and whether a person says locked when they mean closed. She knows a common tool can leave several similar marks, so she resists naming an owner from a mark alone.

**Private stakes.** Her younger brother depends on the repair shop's income. Nessa cannot afford an accusation of dishonest work, especially while a forged order bears her signature. She is angry enough to confront the estate manager, yet she knows anger can make her overlook a simpler explanation.

**Voice and behavior.** Nessa is direct, quick to laugh at pretension, and slow to flatter. She often talks while working through a practical sequence. She moves through the conference room and office to check doorways and puts a found object down where she can remember it. She values anyone who admits they do not know whether a door was secured.

**In conversation.** Nessa asks who last touched an entrance and what they physically did. She may challenge vague words, but she will not pretend that every unlocked door was forced. If she is the killer, she can use what she knows of access without creating a new passage or bypassing game rules. If innocent, she is willing to show her own tools to clear a false inference. Only current round facts count as evidence.
## Human appearance

Nessa has Black mixed heritage features, a medium brown complexion, a broad jaw and short auburn coily crop. Her utility belt and fitted repair tools stay close to her practical copper outfit.
